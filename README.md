# MegaTools Programmatic SEO Hub (All 6 Traffic Giants)

A Next.js App Router + Tailwind CSS programmatic SEO (pSEO) utility empire built around the **6 highest-volume non-finance, non-outdoor consumer search verticals** (41M+ combined monthly search ceiling).

## Included Tool Suites & pSEO Engines

1. **Body Stats, TDEE, Macro & Strength Standards (`/fitness` & `/fitness/[slug]`)**
   - Mifflin-St Jeor BMR/TDEE, Calorie Deficit Goal Date Predictor, Optimal Protein Macro Split, and 1-Rep Max Barbell Strength Standards (Bench, Squat, Deadlift, OHP) by bodyweight.
   - Pre-rendered Height × Weight matrix pages (e.g., `/fitness/5-ft-9-175-lbs`, `/fitness/5-ft-10-180-lbs`) + dynamic fallback parser for any `X-ft-Y-Z-lbs` URL.

2. **Kitchen, Air Fryer & Ingredient Density Converter (`/kitchen` & `/kitchen/[slug]`)**
   - Exact Air Fryer Time & Temp Lookup, Oven-to-Air-Fryer Converter (-25°F & -20% time), and Grams-to-Cups/Tbsp/Tsp Converter by specific ingredient density.
   - Pre-rendered food & ingredient pages (e.g., `/kitchen/chicken-wings`, `/kitchen/salmon-fillet`, `/kitchen/150-grams-flour-to-cups`, `/kitchen/100-grams-butter-to-cups`).

3. **Work Hours, Payroll Decimal & 90-Min REM Sleep Calculator (`/time` & `/time/[slug]`)**
   - Hours Between Two Times with unpaid lunch deduction, ADP/Gusto payroll decimal converter, 90-minute REM sleep cycle bedtime calculator, and business day counter.
   - Pre-rendered shift duration pages (e.g., `/time/8-30-am-to-5-00-pm`, `/time/9-00-am-to-5-00-pm`).

4. **Final Exam Grade & Interactive AP Score Curve Calculators (`/grades` & `/grades/[slug]`)**
   - "What Do I Need on My Final?" calculator + interactive Multiple-Choice & Free-Response sliders for 10 major AP exams.
   - Pre-rendered AP Exam and Final Grade scenario pages (e.g., `/grades/ap-biology`, `/grades/ap-us-history`, `/grades/85-percent-with-20-percent-final`).

5. **Copy-Paste Unicode Fonts, Invisible Character & Word Speaking Time (`/text` & `/text/[slug]`)**
   - 8 live Unicode font transforms, one-click Invisible Characters (`U+2800`, `U+3164`, `U+200B`) for Discord/Fortnite/TikTok, aesthetic symbols board, and Words-to-Speaking-Time calculator.
   - Pre-rendered word count speaking time pages (e.g., `/text/500-words-speaking-time`, `/text/1000-words-speaking-time`).

6. **Interactive Spin Wheels & 5-Letter Word Pattern Solver (`/games` & `/games/[slug]`)**
   - Customizable Spin-the-Wheel Picker (Dinner Wheel, Yes/No, Truth or Dare, Movie Genre) + 5-letter Wordle/Scrabble word finder by starting, ending, included, and excluded letters.
   - Pre-rendered word pattern and wheel pages (e.g., `/games/5-letter-words-starting-with-st`, `/games/what-to-eat-for-dinner`).

7. **pSEO Matrix & Revenue Simulator (`/seo-matrix` & `/sitemap.xml`)**
   - Interactive Display Ad RPM + Affiliate conversion simulator and complete index of all statically generated routes.
